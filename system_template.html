04 / What has been explained?

Component descriptions and system explanations answer different questions.

  • Available: every weight, bias, activation rule, association label, and normalization value.
  • Explained by this trace: which final-hidden readout units contribute at the chosen states, which contributions cancel, and where the two shocks interact.
  • Still needed for a general explanation: a compact account of how those routes change across states and how their combined computation relates to the economic decision process.

Move capital or change the shock states. The labels remain the same while activations, contributions, and cancellations can change. Having names for the parts does not supply that state-dependent account.

This demonstration supplies exact neural accounting for selected comparisons. It does not show that a general explanation is impossible, measure human understanding, or establish that larger models are harder. We do not treat mixed correlations as proof of superposition.

The economic source and the scope of the circuit

The household chooses consumption, positive work effort, and investment under productivity and work-cost shocks. Capital installation costs connect today’s choices to future capital.
