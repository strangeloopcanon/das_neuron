Gate and readout guide

Compute each preactivation p. Apply ReLU to get h. After an edit, downstream preactivations can cross zero. Recalculate downstream gates.

For H2 attenuation, the normalized output change is −fraction × h2[unit] × W3[unit, output]. For an H1 edit, propagate through W2, recalculate H2, then apply W3. Incoming-weight edits require a new forward pass.

Normalized output differences already use TRAIN output SD units. Multiply by output_std to convert a difference to physical units.
