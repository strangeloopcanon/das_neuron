Training and primary tests use positive singletons and zeros. Pairs are a distribution shift. Both full and compressed models have pair errors; interference is not exclusively caused by compression.

From learned weights to a smaller model

First, a tied 6 → 6 → ReLU 6 network learns all six responses. A random QR projection maps its learned encoder into two dimensions. The new bias starts at zero. Fine-tuning then adjusts 18 parameters, down from 42.

The retained vectors form a roughly regular pentagon without a forced polygon. The weak vector stays near zero. Its output is a biased, nearly constant response around 0.15, not an exact zero.
