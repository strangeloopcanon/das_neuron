04 / The economic system

Two added mechanisms.

Work responds to its reward and cost.

χeηnφ = c−σ(1−α)y/n

Labour n is chosen. A shock η changes the cost of working. Productivity z changes the output from work.

Fast capital installation uses resources.

Φ(k,i) = ½κk(i/k−δ)²
q = 1 + κ(i/k−δ)

Investment i builds tomorrow’s capital. Installation costs Φ rise when the investment rate differs from replacement investment.

The full nonlinear equilibrium equations
y = zkαn1−α
c + i + Φ(k,i) = y
k′ = (1−δ)k + i
c−σq = βE[c′−σ(αy′/k′ + (1−δ)q′ − Φk′)]
Φk|i fixed = ½κ[δ² − (i/k)²]

The household maximizes expected discounted CRRA consumption utility minus χeηn1+φ/(1+φ). Competitive firms pay rental rate αy/k and wage (1−α)y/n. Households own capital and pay its installation costs.

Work effort is positive and has no time cap. Investment may be negative. The reported core states have positive consumption and q, and interior next capital. Reference labour .33 describes the deterministic steady state, not the stochastic average.

These networks approximate a numerical policy. They are not exact equivalents of the economic system. This enlargement does not extend the earlier observation-only theorem to full-weight understanding.
